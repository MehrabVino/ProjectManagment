# Threat Model

- **Scope and assets:** What user, business, model, game, or operational assets need protection?
- **Data flow and trust boundaries:** Attach a current diagram.
- **Actors and abuse cases:** Include authorized users, attackers, third parties, and automation.
- **Threats and mitigations:** Record likelihood, impact, owner, control, verification, and residual risk.
- **Secrets and dependencies:** Identify storage, rotation, access, SBOM/dependency review and incident path.
- **Privacy:** Identify personal/sensitive data, purpose, retention, access, deletion, and consent requirements.
- **Review trigger:** Revisit after an integration, permission, data-flow, deployment, or model/agent change.
